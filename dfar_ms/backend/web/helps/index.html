User Manual For MSDFAR

Management System Components

The following components are available in the Management System of Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources:

  • Scientific Inspections
  • Fisherman Register
  • Skipper License
  • Skipper License Renew
  • Boat Numbers
  • Boat Register
  • Boat Registration License
  • Boat Cancel Requests
  • National License
  • High Seas License
  • Division Gear Types

How to Login

  1. Browse Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources on Google
  2. Log in to the Web page
  3. Click on the Officer
  4. Click on MSDFAR
  5. Here it shows the Login Interface of the MSDFAR

User Roles

  • Director General
  • Director Management

  • District Offices
  • Fisheries Inspector
  • District Fisheries Inspector
  • Fisherman
  • Administrators

1. Fisherman Registration

  1. Fisherman should sign up with the MSDFAR first
  2. Create username and password for the fisherman
  3. Log in to the system by providing username and password
  4. The following interface will appear
  5. Fisherman can himself enter his details and can register with the system by providing the following details:
    • Name to be printed on the certificate
    • Gender
    • Passport
    • Date of Birth
    • ID (It will be already appeared in the system)
    • Insurance Number
    • Address
    • Blood group
    • Whether a member of the fishing society
    • Fisheries details
    • District and Division
    • Category
    • Recruitment Year and Management Area
  6. Then finally digital signature of the particular fisherman can be attached and click on the update button.

[Image: Fisherman Registration Interface]

DO NOT KEEP ANY FIELDS EMPTY ON THE INTERFACE

2. Skipper License

Filled by District Offices:

  1. Log in with District Office credentials
  2. The interface is as follows:
  3. Click on the button
  4. Select Fisherman ID and Highest Education Qualification
  5. Enter other Qualifications
  6. Select Fisheries Districts
  7. Select Fisheries Division
  8. Educational Qualifications:
    • Select the Institute
    • Select the Program name
    • Enter the training period
    • Select the Date Certified
  9. Click on Save (All the trainings will be displayed in a row)
  10. Form forwarded to District Fisheries Inspector for review
  11. District Fisheries Inspector reviews, checks for mistakes, uploads missing documents, and submits
  12. Form forwarded to Assistant Director (District Office) for approval
  13. Assistant Director approves
  14. Form forwarded to Director Management (Head Office) for approval
  15. Director Management approves
  16. Form forwarded to Director General for final approval
  17. After final approval, skipper pays and District Office uploads slip
  18. Temporary card issued; ID card given after one year

[Image: Skipper License Interface]

3. Skipper License Renewal

Renewed every 5 years following the same procedure as Skipper License.

4. Boat Numbers

Filled by District Offices:

  1. Log in with District Office credentials
  2. Click on Create Boat Numbers
  3. Enter/Search owner from drop-down list
  4. Select relevant yard, boat design, boat type
  5. Enter hull number, boat propelling details, Management Area
  6. Add remarks if needed
  7. Click Submit
  8. Form forwarded to District Fisheries Inspector for review
  9. Assistant Director approves
  10. Director General gives final approval

[Image: Boat Numbers Interface]

5. Boat Registration License

Filled by Fisheries Inspector (temporarily):

  1. Log in with Fisheries Inspector credentials
  2. Click on “Manage Profiles as Fisherman” (red tab)
  3. Click on Boat Registration
  4. Click on Apply
  5. Fill details and submit
  6. Form forwarded to Fisheries Inspector
  7. Search boat number and fisherman’s name on dashboard
  8. Upload necessary files
  9. Form forwarded through approval chain (DFI, Assistant Director, Director Management, Director General)

[Image: Fisheries Inspector Dashboard]

6. Boat Transfer Requests

Filled by District Office:

  1. Log in with District Officer credentials
  2. Click on Create Boat Number Transfer Request
  3. Fill details (boat number, owner, witness name, NIC, sign date, address, remarks)
  4. Click Apply
  5. Form forwarded through approval chain

[Image: Boat Transfer Interface]

7. Licenses

National Licenses

Reviewed by Fisheries Inspector, District Fisheries Inspector, and Assistant Director.

High Seas Licenses

Reviewed through full approval chain.

Note: Division Gear Types should be added before applying.
