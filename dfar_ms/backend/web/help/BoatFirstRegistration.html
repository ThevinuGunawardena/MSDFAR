Boat first registration

1. Introduction

Boat first registration aims to streamline the vessel registration process. It simplifies application submission, document verification, approval, and issuance of the initial registration certificate. This manual provides clear, step-by-step instructions for users to efficiently navigate the system.

2. System Access

• Access to the MSDFAR system

The system can be accessed through Officer → New MSDFAR System by visiting the Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources website.

• Before access to the system, scan & save the following documents.

     A receipt of purchase of the vessel or proof of ownership

     NIC Copy of Owner

    Marine Engineering Certificate in case of mechanical vessel

     Valid Insurance Certificate in case of mechanical vessel

     Receipt of engine purchase or proof of ownership

     An affidavit that the vessel is not previously registered if the date of manufacture is more than 6 months

     If the registration number belongs to another district, the certificate of the concerned Assistant Director that the vessel is not registered in that district.

     Other
